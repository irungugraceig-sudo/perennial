# Perennial

Turning wasted snow into clean cooling for data centers.

**Hackachusetts 2026 — Energy & Climate Tech track**

AI data centers consumed ~17 billion gallons of fresh water in 2023 just for cooling. Meanwhile
snowy cities spend millions trucking snow away as waste. Perennial connects the two: it calculates
a city's snowfall, plans collection and storage, and quantifies the fresh water, energy, and CO2
offset when that stored snow cools a data center through summer. Proven since 2000 by a Swedish
hospital snow-storage plant — now with the software to plan and scale it.
